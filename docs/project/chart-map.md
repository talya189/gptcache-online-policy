# Report chart map

This supporting note records why each report figure exists and what it is
allowed to establish. Historical figures are generated by
`benchmarks/carma/analyze_results.py`; the separately labeled Gate 7 v2
forensic figures are generated by
`artifacts/samples/analysis/gate7-v2-invalid/analyze_gate7_v2_invalid.py`.
Historical figures use a single blue palette root plus neutral baselines; the
v2 forensic figures use a tightly limited blue/orange/neutral palette with
non-color distinctions. All are inspected in the final PDF at stated width.

| Report section | Analytical question | Family / form | Evidence and fields | Supported takeaway and boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Synthetic results | Does CARMA improve whole-trace valid-hit rate over the per-seed stronger LRU/LFU baseline? | Uncertainty & benchmark; faceted dot-and-interval | `aggregate.csv`; paired CARMA-minus-`BEST_BASELINE` means and 95% bootstrap CIs for three workloads | Shift and stationary deltas are positive; pollution whole-trace gain rounds to zero. The zero line remains visible. |
| Synthetic results | Did CARMA clear the preregistered return-phase margin? | Uncertainty & benchmark; labeled dot comparison with gate line | `aggregate.csv`; return-phase CARMA-minus-LRU/LFU/stronger-baseline deltas and CIs | Gate 4 fails despite 100% CARMA return VHR because LFU is already 99.993%. The +5.0 pp gate is drawn explicitly. |
| Sensitivity and ablations | How does absolute VHR change with cache capacity by workload and policy? | Comparison across an ordered axis; 2x2 small multiples | Capacity-sweep plus primary-test summary rows for capacities 20/50/100/200, with CIs and seed counts | CARMA leads most clearly when capacity is constrained and under shift. All panels share an absolute 0–80% scale; non-primary points remain exploratory. |
| Historical real-system evidence | What system costs differ across policies on the actual SQLite/FAISS path? | Comparison; four zero-based bar panels with per-seed points | Five historical manifests/runs per policy; post-embedding p95, policy-only p95, throughput, and sampled peak RSS | Policy-only work is higher for CARMA, while every historical post-embedding diagnostic stays within its numerical limit. Precomputed vectors exclude embedding generation, so this original Gate 7 generation remains `PENDING`. |
| Gate 7 v2 invalid evidence | What do the full-request and post-embedding latency distributions look like for CARMA, LRU, and LFU when every frozen seed has equal weight? | Distribution; two-panel quantile-derived ECDF approximation, with a linear full-request axis and labeled log post-embedding axis | `artifacts/samples/analysis/gate7-v2-invalid/latency-quantile-ecdf.csv`; 1,001 Type-7 quantiles for 15,000 requests per policy and metric | ONNX embedding dominates full-path latency and the policy tails differ. The figure is prominently labeled `INVALID / NONCLAIMABLE`; it supports diagnosis, not a formal policy ranking. |
| Gate 7 v2 invalid supplement | Which frozen CARMA/LRU systems checks would pass or fail by seed if the measurements were structurally admissible? | Forensic comparison; annotated 5-by-5 binary matrix with exact values and non-color PASS/FAIL labels | `artifacts/samples/analysis/gate7-v2-invalid/per-seed-checks.csv`; five seeds by five frozen checks | The bundle descriptively passes 24/25 checks; seed 20261001 fails the p95 absolute delta. This matrix is retained as supplementary evidence rather than embedded in the report, and cannot establish a formal Gate 7 result. |

For the historical analyzer figures, PNG/SVG dimensions, minimum source font,
scaled report font, and output hashes are recorded in the analyzer result.
Tests fail if a visible text artist drops below the 12.5 pt source contract or
if the analyzer's nominal 7.05-inch render would fall below 10 pt. The final
6.80-inch render scales each 8.0-inch historical figure's minimum label to
10.625 pt. Both Gate 7 v2 figures use approximately 7.05-inch source canvases
with no source text below 11.5 pt. The latency distribution is embedded from
its vector PDF at full report width; the matrix remains supplementary. Their
chart contracts and QA notes are retained in
`artifacts/samples/analysis/gate7-v2-invalid/chart-contract.md`. Final PDF page
count and visual inspection are recorded separately after the report rebuild.
